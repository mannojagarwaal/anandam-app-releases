# Anandam app for Android

**Anandam** is the app of [Anandam Foundation – The Ocean of Bliss](https://anandamfoundation.org):
Thought of the Day, guided meditation, live sessions and talks with **Anando**, courses and
community – free; contribution is always voluntary.

> **Beta for testing.** This version is for Anandam's inner circle. Payments in the app are in
> **test mode – no real money is charged.**

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/mannojagarwaal/anandam-app-releases/releases)

## How to install (3 steps)

1. **Download** – tap the link above on your Android phone. If the phone warns about the file,
   tap **Download anyway** (it is our own app).
2. **Allow installing** – open the downloaded file. If Android asks, tap **Settings** and turn
   on **Allow from this source** ("Install unknown apps") for Chrome, WhatsApp or Files, then go
   back.
3. **Install and open** – tap **Install**, then **Open** (if Play Protect asks, choose **Install
   anyway**). Sign in with your email – you get a 6-digit code.

Updates: small updates come inside the app by themselves; for a new version, download again
from the same link and install over the old app – nothing is lost.
